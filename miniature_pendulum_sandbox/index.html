Privacy Policy for Miniature Pendulum Sandbox

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you install, access, or use the Miniature Pendulum Sandbox browser extension.

Miniature Pendulum Sandbox is a compact physics simulation extension that runs within a browser toolbar popup. It allows users to adjust pendulum length and mass, swing the pendulum, and observe how it interacts with and knocks over stacked shapes.

The extension is designed as a local interactive sandbox. Its core physics simulation, collision calculations, object movement, and visual rendering take place within the browser environment.

Privacy Summary

Miniature Pendulum Sandbox does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to use its core functionality.

Pendulum length, pendulum mass, object positions, collision states, and other simulation parameters are used solely to run the requested physics sandbox.

The extension is designed to operate locally through its toolbar popup and does not require general access to webpage content for its standard functionality.
